# Governance

croft is an early project, and this document stays small until it has more contributors.

## Maintainers

Maintainers, listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), review and merge changes, cut releases and handle security reports.

A contributor with sustained, reviewed contributions can be nominated by a maintainer. Existing maintainers approve by majority. A maintainer can step down at any time, and one inactive for six months moves to emeritus after notice.

## Decisions

Everyday decisions happen in issues and pull requests by lazy consensus. Every change needs a review from a maintainer other than its author, once the project has two or more maintainers.

Changes to this document, to the license or to the project's direction need a majority of maintainers, in a public issue open for at least seven days.

## Code of Conduct

Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
